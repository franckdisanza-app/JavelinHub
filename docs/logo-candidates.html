One thing your sketch forced, and it is worth knowing

A long javelin at 34° does not fit a square frame alongside a full-height athlete. The arithmetic is unforgiving: a shaft rises 0.559× its own length, so at 34° a 62-unit javelin needs 35 units of vertical rise and 52 of width. In a 48×48 box with a 38-tall figure there is only about 10 units of headroom — which caps the javelin at 17.9 units. That is precisely the stubby shaft I drew last time, and it is why it looked wrong.

The frame has to be at least 1.36:1 landscape. Yours is 4:3, which is exactly why it works. So the mark is now two drawings: a wide one for the header and anywhere with room, and a square crop for the favicon and app icon, where the javelin is necessarily shortened.
